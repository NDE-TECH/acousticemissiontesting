About IAIS

Inspection support from India and Abu Dhabi

Learn about the IAIS India and IAIS UAE entities and how to share an acoustic emission testing requirement.

Integrity and advanced inspection across India and the UAE

Integrity & Advanced Inspection Solutions India Pvt. Ltd. is the India entity of the IAIS group. Its published portfolio covers industrial NDT, advanced inspection, metallurgical and testing work, and related technical services for asset owners and project teams. The India operation is based in the Chennai region and supports enquiries for work in India and other markets.

Integrity & Advanced Inspection Solutions UAE LLC is the Abu Dhabi entity. Its published service portfolio brings inspection, asset-integrity and engineering support closer to clients in the UAE and GCC. The UAE operation draws on the wider IAIS group’s technical resources while developing project scopes suited to regional facilities and requirements.

These are distinct entities. For a specific enquiry, the responsible contracting entity, project location, mobilization plan and deliverables are agreed in the proposal.

What our wider inspection work contributes

01 · METHOD SELECTION

Start with the technical question

Inspection methods are selected around material, geometry, access, likely damage mechanism, test conditions and the decision the client needs. The UAE service architecture includes conventional and advanced NDT, tank and pressure-equipment integrity, piping, structural inspection and materials work.

02 · COMPLEMENTARY EVIDENCE

Connect screening with confirmation

AET can identify activity under defined conditions. When a source needs characterization, the project may require local ultrasonic, surface, visual or other examination. The method combination and personnel are specified only after reviewing the particular asset.

03 · PROJECT CONTROL

Plan, perform and report

A useful inspection scope identifies its standard, procedure, access, operating window, quality checks, coverage, evaluation basis and reporting requirements. It also states limitations and any next examination needed to support an integrity decision.

04 · REGIONAL CONTACT

Enquiries from India and Abu Dhabi

Clients can send project details to the India or UAE office. The appropriate entity and technical team can review feasibility, location and schedule before issuing a formal AET offer.

Why this dedicated acoustic emission site exists

This website explains where acoustic emission testing may help with industrial asset decisions. It brings together the method, possible applications, practical test conditions, limitations and complementary inspection routes in one place so that clients can describe their requirement precisely.

For each enquiry, the IAIS team reviews the asset and confirms the proposed personnel, instrumentation, procedure, schedule and deliverables in the project offer.

For more information on the group’s broader activities, visit IAIS India and IAIS UAE. For this method, start with how AET works, the application guides or a project enquiry.
