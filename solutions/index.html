Solutions

Application-oriented acoustic emission inspection solutions

Explore IAIS AET solution pathways for screening, leak investigation, pressure-test monitoring, targeted follow-up and longer-term monitoring.

Choose the decision you need to make

Screen active zones

Monitor a defined asset during appropriate loading and prioritize localized investigation where validated activity occurs.

Investigate a suspected leak

Evaluate whether a pressure-difference-driven signal can be detected and distinguished from normal operating noise.

Monitor a pressure test

Record acoustic activity against an approved pressure-test sequence, then investigate relevant sources within the governing acceptance process.

Trend an operating asset

Use a repeat or extended baseline to identify changes in activity, with instrument checks and agreed alarm response.

Evaluate a composite asset

Apply material-specific loading and interpretation to an FRP tank, vessel or other relevant component.

Support a structural decision

Correlate active signals with known loads and follow them with structural engineering and localized NDT.

A solution is a sequence, not a single instrument reading

IAIS can discuss a project-specific route from objective and feasibility to an agreed AET method, data review and complementary inspection. Whether a particular field service can be offered is confirmed in the proposal after personnel, equipment, access, standard and site constraints are checked.

For example, a tank concern may begin with an operating review and sensor-coverage plan, then move to monitored filling and a mapped follow-up using the method appropriate to the affected region. A pressure-vessel question may use a controlled loading cycle and then targeted UT or PAUT if a source requires characterization. Neither sequence promises detection of every defect.
