Which method answers which question?

AET: Is something active?

Monitors acoustic events during the defined condition and may prioritize a source zone.

UT / PAUT / TOFD: What is the discontinuity?

Targeted ultrasonic methods may locate, characterize or size a flaw where geometry and procedure permit.

Leak test: Is the boundary tight?

A specified tightness or leak-rate method establishes the required leak acceptance result.

Engineering assessment: What action follows?

Combines inspection evidence with design, loading, damage mechanism and applicable criteria.
