Why acoustic emission

Wide-area listening, followed by targeted inspection

AE sensors listen for transient elastic waves generated by active processes in a material or structure. Activity can help identify areas that deserve closer investigation, while the final interpretation remains tied to the asset, coverage, noise environment and test procedure.

Start with feasibility, not assumptions

Share drawings, dimensions, material, service conditions, known degradation and the available loading window. The applicable procedure and sensor plan depend on those details.
